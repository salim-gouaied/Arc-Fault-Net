\section{Recording-level GroupKFold: per-fold details}

Each fold holds out entire recordings (all cycles of given experiments). The strong inter-fold dispersion reflects the heterogeneity of the held-out conditions: folds whose test recordings resemble trained conditions are solved perfectly, while folds containing unique load/material combinations concentrate the errors.
